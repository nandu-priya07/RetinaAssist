About the research

Why RetinaAssist?

Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of preventable vision loss. Screening programmes generate large volumes of retinal images that specialists must review. RetinaAssist explores how explainable AI could help teams organise that review workload — without replacing clinical judgement.

Insights & responsible AI

How to read the results

All five scores, every time

Each prediction shows scores for all five classes, so close calls are visible — not just the top grade.

Influence, not proof

Grad-CAM highlights regions that influenced the model output. It does not confirm lesions.

Flags draw attention

Image-quality and uncertainty flags help identify cases that may need a closer look.

Clinicians decide

Clinicians remain responsible for reviewing findings and making every decision.
