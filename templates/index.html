Explainability

See what the model responded to

For every uploaded image, RetinaAssist computes a Grad-CAM map from the final convolutional layer of the model and shows it next to the original photograph — so reviewers can judge whether the model's attention is plausible.

  • Real outputs only. Analysis pages show the uploaded image and the Grad-CAM generated for it — never artwork.
  • Honest framing. Heatmaps show model influence; they do not confirm lesions or a diagnosis.
  • Clinician decides. Reviewers record agreement or disagreement, and every action is audited.

Research disclaimer

RetinaAssist is an academic research prototype. Its predictions are not diagnoses, its class scores are not calibrated disease probabilities, and it has not been externally or clinically validated. It does not recommend treatment or medication. A real clinical deployment would require external validation, privacy safeguards, a security review and regulatory assessment.
